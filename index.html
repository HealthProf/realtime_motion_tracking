    Layers
      More anatomy

      All anatomy layers are a generic schematic for an average adult, fitted to the tracked joints. They show roughly where structures lie, not this person's anatomy. Organ positions in particular vary with breathing, posture and build. Moving highlights on vessels show the direction of blood flow at a nominal rate: they are not a measurement of this person's pulse or circulation, and muscles are not shown as active or inactive.
