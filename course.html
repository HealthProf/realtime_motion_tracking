    How it works

    1. Choose your pair of clips.
    2. Open an analysis. Scrub the clip to a moment and press Capture snapshot.
    3. Draw and write on the snapshot with the tools. Name it and pick the prompt it answers.
    4. For the comparison, line both clips up at the same moment and press Lock, then write over the playing videos.
    5. Export a PDF (snapshots) or a video (comparison) and upload it to your course site.

    Your work saves on this device as you go. Nothing is uploaded. Download a backup to move it to another device.
